02 / REFERENCE

What’s in the data

FIELD GUIDE

The snapshot targets leading assets by market cap. Refreshes run on a schedule and may be delayed. Rankings can change between requests, so the order is approximate.

FieldMeaning
idCoinGecko asset ID
market_cap_rankMarket-cap rank
name, symbolAsset name and ticker
current_priceUSD price
market_capUSD market capitalization
fully_diluted_valuationUSD fully diluted valuation
price_change_percentage_24hRolling 24-hour price change (%)
last_updatedSource update time

Missing values are blank in CSV and null in JSON. JSON includes the snapshot completion time. Data is not investment advice.

03 / CONNECT YOUR TOOLS

Bring it to your spreadsheet.

In Google Sheets, paste this formula into a cell:

=IMPORTDATA("URL_TO_ASSETS_CSV")

In Excel, choose Data → From Web and enter the CSV URL. Spreadsheet refresh timing is independent of fetchr.
