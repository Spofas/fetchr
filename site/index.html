What’s included

The snapshot targets the leading assets by market capitalization. Its actual asset count and refresh time appear above. It is refreshed on a schedule when the workflow runs successfully; scheduled runs may be delayed. Rankings can change between source requests, so the ranking is approximate.

FieldMeaning
idCoinGecko asset ID
market_cap_rankMarket-cap rank
name, symbolAsset name and ticker
current_priceUSD price
market_capUSD market capitalization
fully_diluted_valuationUSD fully diluted valuation
price_change_percentage_24hRolling 24-hour price change (%)
last_updatedSource update time

Missing data is blank in CSV and null in JSON. JSON includes the snapshot completion time. Values come from CoinGecko and are not investment advice.

Use in a spreadsheet

In Google Sheets, enter =IMPORTDATA("URL_TO_ASSETS_CSV"). In Excel, choose Data → From Web and enter the CSV URL. Spreadsheet refresh timing is independent of fetchr.
